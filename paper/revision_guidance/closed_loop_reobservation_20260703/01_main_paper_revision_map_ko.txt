2. Method / Evidence graph policy
추천 수정 위치
- paper/sections/03_method_draft.tex
- targeted re-observation action을 설명하는 문단 직후

추천 수정
- targeted_reobserve가 "바로 최종 class를 확정하는 action"이 아니라,
  missing view 또는 high ambiguity hypothesis에 대해 추가 evidence를 요청하는
  graph action이라는 점을 더 명확히 한다.
- 현재 결과는 fresh commanded capture이지만 autonomous physical flight가 아니므로
  "closed-loop camera-pose/evidence-update validation"이라고 써야 한다.

삽입 후보 문장
"For the current validation, the action is evaluated as a closed-loop
camera-pose/evidence update: routed hypotheses generate targeted Isaac/Cesium
camera poses, the resulting detector tokens are matched back to the hypotheses,
the graph support and ambiguity terms are recomputed, and the policy is
re-applied to measure whether the action changes."

evidence-completion role of the feedback branch."

5. Conclusion
추천 수정
- system validation의 약점을 숨기지 말고, controlled detector + evidence graph
  + fresh commanded recapture + real-Cesium handoff로 정리한다.
- future work에는 "autonomous flight control and larger scenario count"를
  넣는 편이 좋다.

결론 삽입 후보 문장
"The MarineCity validation remains intentionally bounded, but the added fresh
commanded recapture shows that the graph feedback branch can absorb
re-observation evidence and reduce ambiguous actions rather than merely
listing re-observation requests."
