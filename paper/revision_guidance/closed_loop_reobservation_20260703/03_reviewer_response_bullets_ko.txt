Reviewer response bullet draft

Reviewer concern
"The paper routes ambiguous hypotheses to targeted re-observation, but it does
not show whether re-observation is actually executed or whether it improves the
graph state."

Response bullet 1
We added a fresh commanded Isaac/Cesium camera-pose/evidence-update diagnostic
for the nine MarineCity hypotheses selected by the targeted re-observation
policy.

Response bullet 2
The diagnostic generates targeted camera poses from the evidence graph, captures
fresh Isaac/Cesium RGB/depth frames, runs the detector on those frames, matches
the resulting EvidenceTokens back to the routed hypotheses, recomputes graph
support/ambiguity, and reapplies the action policy.

Response bullet 3
Six of the nine routed hypotheses receive matching same-class re-observation
evidence and transition from targeted_reobserve to monitor. Three remain in
targeted re-observation because no matching same-class recapture token is
available.

Response bullet 4
Mean closed-loop ambiguity decreases from 0.923 to 0.775. This shows that the
feedback branch can reduce ambiguity when missing-view evidence is supplied,
rather than merely reporting re-observation as an unexecuted action.

Response bullet 5
We explicitly scope the result as commanded camera-pose/evidence-update
validation in Isaac/Cesium. We do not claim a completed autonomous physical UAV
planner or large-scale closed-loop flight benchmark.
