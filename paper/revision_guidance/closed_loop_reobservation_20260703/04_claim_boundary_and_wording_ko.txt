Claim boundary and wording guide

안전한 표현
- fresh commanded Isaac/Cesium recapture
- closed-loop camera-pose/evidence-update validation
- real-Cesium recapture EvidenceTokens
- targeted re-observation branch validation
- graph recomputation after follow-up evidence
- action transition from targeted_reobserve to monitor
- bounded system validation

본문에서 사용 가능한 문장
"The closed-loop result validates the camera-pose/evidence-update branch:
graph-generated target poses produce fresh Isaac/Cesium recapture tokens that
reduce graph ambiguity and change the policy decision for matched hypotheses."

"We do not claim a full autonomous physical UAV planner; the current
contribution is the evidence contract between detector tokens, graph ambiguity,
graph-generated camera poses, and re-observation-triggered evidence completion."

조심해야 하는 표현
- autonomous re-observation completed
- fully closed-loop UAV control
- real-time multi-UAV planner
- full 3D benchmark
- LLM verifier benchmark
- SOTA closed-loop robotic perception

금지에 가까운 표현
"Our system autonomously commands UAVs to recapture all ambiguous objects."
현재 산출물만으로는 이 표현을 쓰면 안 된다.

"The closed-loop planner resolves all ambiguous hypotheses."
실제로는 9개 중 6개만 monitor로 전환되고 3개는 remain targeted_reobserve이다.

"The 3D/LLM modules are fully evaluated."
현재 논문 전략상 3D/LLM은 bounded handoff/interface validation으로 두는 편이
안전하다.

최종 권장 claim
"The added fresh commanded recapture closes the most visible gap in the current
paper: it shows that targeted re-observation is not only an action label, but
an evidence-update path that can reduce ambiguity when matching recapture
evidence is available."
