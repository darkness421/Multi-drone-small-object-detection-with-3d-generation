Supplementary 수정 지도

주의
이 문서는 supplementary에 넣을 closed-loop 보강 내용을 정리한 것이다. 기존
supplementary_patch_bundle.tex는 직접 수정하지 않았다.

추천 삽입 위치
paper/sections/supp_marinecity_system_details.tex

가장 자연스러운 위치
1. "Re-observation-policy analysis." 문단 직후
2. "Graph construction and decision-policy details." 문단 직전

이유
기존 supplementary는 17개 graph hypothesis에 대해 action policy가 9개를
targeted re-observation으로 보낸다는 점까지만 설명한다. 따라서 그 직후에
"그 9개 중 재관측 evidence가 들어왔을 때 어떻게 바뀌는가"를 보여주는 것이
리뷰어 입장에서 가장 흐름이 좋다.

서플 삽입 후보 문단
"We add a closed-loop evidence-update diagnostic for the nine hypotheses
selected by the targeted re-observation action. The diagnostic uses fresh
commanded Isaac/Cesium camera poses generated from the graph target plan and
therefore evaluates camera-pose/evidence-update closure rather than a fully
autonomous physical UAV planner. A recapture token is considered useful when
it matches the routed class and missing-view request within the graph matching
gate. Six of the nine routed hypotheses receive such evidence and transition
from targeted re-observation to monitor after graph recomputation; three remain
queued because no matching same-class recapture token is available. Mean
closed-loop ambiguity decreases from 0.923 to 0.775."

서플 caption 후보 - contact sheet
"Closed-loop re-observation evidence update for the nine MarineCity hypotheses
routed to targeted re-observation. Each panel shows the before state, the
fresh commanded recapture evidence when available, and the recomputed action.
Six hypotheses receive additional real-Cesium evidence and move to monitor,
while three remain in the targeted re-observation queue due to missing
same-class follow-up evidence."

서플 caption 후보 - ambiguity plot
"Before/after ambiguity under targeted re-observation evidence. Matching
real-Cesium recapture tokens reduce ambiguity for six hypotheses, while
hypotheses without matching same-class tokens remain unchanged and are kept in
the re-observation queue."

서플에서 같이 써야 하는 한계 문장
"Because this diagnostic commands Isaac/Cesium camera poses but does not execute
autonomous physical UAV flight control, we report it as closed-loop
camera-pose/evidence-update validation rather than as a completed autonomous
planner benchmark."
