\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

UAV small-object perception is important for traffic monitoring, disaster
response, maritime safety, urban inspection, and search-and-rescue. In these
settings, the targets of interest often occupy only a few pixels, appear under
large altitude and viewpoint changes, and are easily confused with shadows,
buildings, vegetation, water texture, or dense nearby objects. The difficulty is
not only that 2D detection is hard. A single detection rarely provides enough
evidence for a fine-grained object decision: a pedestrian may resemble debris
from a top-down view, a vehicle may be partially hidden by urban clutter, and a
boat-like object may be confused with wave reflection. Multi-UAV observation can
help resolve such cases, but only if heterogeneous views are aligned and their
uncertainty is represented explicitly.

Most UAV detection systems still treat the detector output as the final answer.
Recent small-object detectors improve shallow feature usage, spatial-frequency
response, and lightweight multi-scale fusion
\cite{hu2024universalyolo,jiang2024mffsodnet,bai2025sffefyolo,chao2025bpdyolo,hu2025csfprrtdetr,yang2026uavdet}.
These advances are valuable, but they do not by themselves answer a system-level
question: what should a cooperative UAV team do when the 2D evidence is
ambiguous, conflicting, or missing? Conversely, 3D reconstruction and neural
rendering methods can provide geometric context, but they are rarely connected
to the uncertainty and object-level evidence produced by small-object detectors.

We address this gap with \textbf{CoM3D-ACE}, an ambiguity-centric cooperative
multi-UAV perception framework. The core idea is to turn 2D detections into
structured \emph{EvidenceTokens}, associate them across views into a 3D evidence
graph, and route only high-ambiguity object hypotheses to 3D completion,
targeted re-observation, and a graph-grounded reasoner. The 2D frontend is
\textbf{SAFR-YOLO}, a UAV small-object detector built around high-resolution
P2/P3/P4 heads, SelfAttnFR refinement, TinyFReLU activation, and an
EvidenceToken output interface. The 3D and reasoning stages are evaluated as a
real-Cesium MarineCity system protocol, where UAV views, detector outputs,
cross-view associations, and neural-3D runner-family smoke metrics are logged
under a controlled pipeline.

Our current main detector result is fixed under a normalized VisDrone2019-DET
1280-pixel, three-seed protocol. SAFR-YOLO improves AP/AP50 over the strongest
stock YOLO baseline in our controlled comparison while using fewer parameters
than YOLOv11l. We deliberately separate this completed detector claim from the
MarineCity 3D/reasoner claims. The MarineCity experiments validate real-Cesium
scene loading, multi-UAV capture, detector-token export, 3D evidence grouping,
and runner-family neural-3D execution; they are not presented as a completed
large-scale 3D reconstruction benchmark.

Our contributions are:

\begin{itemize}
    \item We propose \textbf{SAFR-YOLO}, a high-resolution UAV small-object
    evidence generator that combines P2/P3/P4 detection heads, SelfAttnFR
    refinement, TinyFReLU activation, and overlap-aware EvidenceToken export.
    \item We introduce an ambiguity-centric multi-UAV evidence graph that
    converts detections into cross-view object hypotheses with support,
    conflict, missing-evidence, and uncertainty cues.
    \item We construct a real-Cesium MarineCity system protocol for cooperative
    UAV evidence completion, including 3-UAV captures, detector overlays,
    cross-view graph artifacts, and neural-3D runner-family smoke metrics.
    \item We validate the detector with a normalized three-seed VisDrone
    comparison and provide supplementary coverage for full YOLO-family scales,
    cited UAV small-object methods, ablations, heat maps, and robustness
    analyses.
\end{itemize}
