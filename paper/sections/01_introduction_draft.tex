\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

UAV small-object perception supports traffic monitoring, disaster response,
maritime safety, urban inspection, and search-and-rescue
\cite{hu2024universalyolo,jiang2024mffsodnet,sadeghibakhi2025comparison}.
Targets often occupy only a few pixels, change appearance with altitude and
viewpoint, and are confused with shadows, buildings, vegetation, water texture,
or nearby objects. The difficulty is not only 2D detection: a single detection
rarely gives enough evidence for a fine-grained decision. A pedestrian may
resemble debris, a vehicle may be hidden by urban clutter, and a boat-like
object may be confused with wave reflection. Multi-UAV observation can resolve
such cases only when heterogeneous views are aligned and uncertainty is made
explicit \cite{zhang2024attention4align,cai2025neusis}.

Most UAV detection systems still treat the detector output as the final answer.
Recent small-object detectors improve shallow feature usage, spatial-frequency
response, and lightweight multi-scale fusion
\cite{hu2024universalyolo,jiang2024mffsodnet,bai2025sffefyolo,chao2025bpdyolo,hu2025csfprrtdetr,yang2026uavdet}.
These advances are valuable, but they do not by themselves answer a system-level
question of how a cooperative UAV team should act when 2D evidence is
ambiguous, conflicting, or incomplete. Conversely, 3D reconstruction and neural
rendering can provide geometric context, but they are rarely connected to
detector uncertainty and object-level small-object evidence.

We address this gap with \textbf{CoM3D-ACE}, an ambiguity-centric cooperative
multi-UAV perception framework. The core idea is to turn 2D detections into
structured \emph{EvidenceTokens}, associate them across views into a 3D evidence
graph, and send only high-ambiguity object hypotheses to 3D completion,
targeted re-observation, and a graph-grounded reasoner. The 2D frontend is
\textbf{SAFR-YOLO}, a UAV small-object detector built around high-resolution
P2/P3/P4 heads, SelfAttnFR refinement, TinyFReLU activation, and an
EvidenceToken output interface. The 3D and reasoning stages are evaluated as a
real-Cesium MarineCity system protocol, where UAV views, detector outputs,
cross-view associations, and neural-3D runner-family validation metrics are logged
under a controlled pipeline.

The detector result is fixed under a normalized VisDrone2019-DET 1280-pixel,
three-seed protocol. SAFR-YOLO improves AP/AP50 over the strongest stock YOLO
baseline in our controlled comparison while using fewer parameters than
YOLOv11l. The MarineCity experiments separately validate real-Cesium scene
loading, multi-UAV capture, detector-token export, 3D evidence grouping, and
runner-family neural-3D execution; they are not presented as a completed
large-scale 3D reconstruction benchmark.

Our contributions are threefold:

\begin{itemize}
    \item We propose \textbf{SAFR-YOLO}, a high-resolution UAV small-object
    evidence generator that combines P2/P3/P4 detection heads, SelfAttnFR
    refinement, TinyFReLU activation, and overlap-aware EvidenceToken export,
    and validate it under normalized three-seed VisDrone comparisons.
    \item We introduce an ambiguity-centric multi-UAV evidence graph that
    converts detections into cross-view object hypotheses with support,
    conflict, missing-evidence, and uncertainty cues for selective 3D
    completion and re-observation.
    \item We construct a real-Cesium MarineCity system protocol for cooperative
    UAV evidence completion, including 3-UAV captures, detector overlays,
    cross-view graph artifacts, neural-3D runner-family validation metrics, and
    graph-grounded reasoner validation records.
\end{itemize}
