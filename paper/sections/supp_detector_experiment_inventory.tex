\paragraph{Protocol-mismatched related-work checks.}
CSFPR-RTDETR, LEAF-YOLO-S, and LEAF-YOLO-N have repository-backed 640-pixel
evaluations. They should be shown in a separate supplementary table with the
input-resolution note visible in the method name or caption. They should not be
merged into the main detector comparison until a normalized 1280-pixel protocol
or a defensible cross-resolution comparison is completed.

\paragraph{Module-search rows.}
Single-seed DCT, wavelet, CBAM, NMS, and activation-function probes should be
reported as a design-search appendix, not as competing final methods. The final
ablation table should be restricted to interpretable components of
SAFR-YOLO: high-resolution P2/P3/P4 heads, TinySpatialFReLU, SelfAttnFR,
and the final NMS setting.
