\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented CoM3D-ACE, an ambiguity-centric cooperative multi-UAV perception
framework that connects UAV small-object detection, cross-view 3D evidence, and
graph-grounded reasoning. The 2D frontend, SAFR-YOLO, improves the normalized
three-seed VisDrone detector comparison while using fewer parameters than the
strongest stock YOLO baseline in our table. The ablation results show that this
gain is not due to a single isolated change: high-resolution P2/P3/P4 heads
recover most of the small-object signal, SelfAttnFR refinement improves the
final AP/AP50/F1 balance, and the EvidenceToken interface turns detector
outputs into a form that can be consumed by the cooperative system.

Beyond the detector, the MarineCity real-Cesium protocol demonstrates that
multi-UAV captures, detector EvidenceTokens, cross-view evidence graphs, and
neural-3D runner-family smoke metrics can be integrated into a single
evidence-completion pipeline. The current 3D rows are deliberately framed as
system feasibility evidence: Nerfacto, Instant-NGP, and a 3DGS-style runner can
all consume the captured scene package, while their PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS/FPS values
make the quality--speed trade-off visible. The qualitative MarineCity figure
then verifies that the numerical rows correspond to real scene captures and
detector outputs rather than a proxy scene.

The current system results are intentionally reported conservatively. We claim
the 2D detector result as the completed quantitative contribution and report
MarineCity as real-Cesium system/protocol validation rather than as a finished
large-scale 3D reconstruction benchmark. This conservative split is important:
it keeps the main claim self-contained while still showing how the proposed
detector, 3D graph, completion runners, and graph-grounded reasoner are wired
for cooperative UAV perception. Future work will extend the MarineCity capture
set, add longer optimized 3D validation, and broaden AeroGraph response
evaluation before promoting the reasoner to a final benchmark claim.
