This supplementary material expands the UAV detector comparisons and system
validation artifacts from the main paper. The cited detector priors include
MFFSODNet~\cite{jiang2024mffsodnet}, SFFEF-YOLO~\cite{bai2025sffefyolo},
BPD-YOLO~\cite{chao2025bpdyolo}, CSFPR-RTDETR~\cite{hu2025csfprrtdetr},
HF-D-FINE~\cite{hu2026hfdifine}, UAVDet~\cite{yang2026uavdet}, and related
UAV small-object methods~\cite{han2025lrds,xie2026dgtsod,wang2026d2adetector,
chen2026ufodetr,bi2025cfia,li2026dfformer,tan2025rtuavsod}. The module and
post-processing discussion follows the activation and NMS literature
where appropriate~\cite{ma2020frelu,bodla2017softnms}, while the graph-reasoner
motivation is grounded in UAV/LLM reasoning studies~\cite{tian2025uavsmeetllms,
cai2025neusis}.
